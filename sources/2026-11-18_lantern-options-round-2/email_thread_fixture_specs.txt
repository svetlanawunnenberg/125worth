Please see attached photometrics and fixture schedule with links to the spec sheets. A few notes –

 

I had to use really low wattage lamps in the lanterns (3w) due to the property line being so close and the town requirements not allowing more than 0.5 footcandles over the property line. Because of this, the loggia is pretty dimly lit… my recommendation is to submit this and install as shown, and if the tenants feel the loggia is too dim you can swap out the lamps later for ones with more wattage. We are around 2 footcandles on the loggia and typically we would want 5-10. Because of this issue, I recommend sticking with the same size lanterns throughout. If we put larger lanterns at the main entrance, they will likely have more lamps (4 instead of 3) and then there will be too much spillage over the property line.
I moved the F2 steplights to the interior side of the columns to help with light over the property line as well.
