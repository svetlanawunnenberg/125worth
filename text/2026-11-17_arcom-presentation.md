## Project summary (from the deck)

The deck proposes a renovation of an existing three-story building at 125 Worth Avenue. The main moves are:

- Replacing the ground-floor columns with **piers**, so the arches end at a pier instead of a column.
- Widening the Worth Avenue **loggia** from about **4'-0" to about 10'-0"**.
- Removing the ground-floor glass **curtain wall** and putting in new **storefronts**.
- Adding retail floor area **below the existing second-floor cantilever**, where there is parking today.
- Replacing windows and doors on floors 2 and 3.
- Upgrading the mechanical penthouse, with a **pergola**, a modified parapet and **45% open screening**.
- Adding a **green space** at the northwest corner.

The ground floor becomes **six retail suites** plus a lobby. Finished floor elevation (F.F.E.) is **+12.64'** throughout.

**Slide 24: Special Allowances & Variances.**
Special allowances (per the Worth Avenue Design Guidelines):
- To permit three stories in the C-WA zoning district.*
- Lot coverage of 57.3% on the first floor and 64.3% on the second and third floors.*
- Eliminating the maximum building length to allow 201.83' instead of 150'.*
- Eliminating the 15,000 SF maximum gross floor area, allowing 9,168 SF over the existing 59,504 SF for a 68,672 SF building.*

Variances:
1. Parking reduced from 261 required spaces to 136 provided.
2. Off-street loading spaces reduced from 3 required to 1 proposed space, backing a loading/staging area.
3. Lot coverage of 64.3% on the third floor instead of the 30% maximum.
4. Landscape open space of 5.9% instead of the 25% minimum (5.9% existing, 15.4% perceived).
5. Exceeding the maximum overall height by 7.75'. The overall building is 47.75', above the 40' allowance.*

\*Existing non-conforming conditions.
