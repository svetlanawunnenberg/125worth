# 125 Worth Avenue: Project Base, Master Index

The searchable knowledge base for the 125 Worth Avenue renovation in Palm Beach, FL. Start here.

## Project at a glance
- **Address:** 125 Worth Avenue, Palm Beach, FL (parcel 0380), on the north side of Worth Ave between 151 Worth Ave (west) and 101 Worth Ave / Kirkland House (east). Across the street: 150 Worth Ave (the Esplanade).
- **Owner:** L3 Capital (contact: Whitney Robinette).
- **Architect:** Fairfax & Sammons Architects (New York and Palm Beach).
- **Lighting consultant:** CX Connect (Raquel Swearingen).
- **Status (Nov 2026):** 50% Design Development. ARCOM / Town Council presentation dated **December 9, 2026**.
- **Scope:** piers replacing columns on Worth Ave; loggia widened from about 4' to about 10'; new storefronts; ground-floor retail infill under the 2nd-floor cantilever; 6 retail suites; window and door replacement on floors 2 and 3; penthouse pergola and mechanical screening; northwest green space.
- **Building:** 3 stories, 68,672 SF proposed (59,504 SF existing), 47.75' overall height, F.F.E. +12.64'.

## Key decisions and constraints
- **Light spill:** no more than 0.5 footcandles over the property line. The lanterns use 3W lamps; the loggia sits at about 2 fc (5 to 10 is typical). Use the same size lantern throughout; avoid 4-lamp fixtures.
- **Lantern style:** Richard wants ornate, authentic Mizner-style lanterns (Remains Lighting, Bonnet House references). Round 1 was rejected as too simplified.
- **Step lights:** recessed or flush in the new piers.
- **Materials:** coquina or concrete stone loggia floor (slab or 3 to 4 ft chevron); Haddonstone cast stone treads; Regts Delft field tile; wood-beam or stucco loggia ceiling; Farrow & Ball Wimborne White No. 239 walls.
